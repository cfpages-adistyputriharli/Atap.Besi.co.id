# Topical Authority — atap.besi.co.id

## Role and boundary

`atap.besi.co.id` should become an Indonesian, national-scope reference and procurement-support hub for profiled metal roof systems: profiles, base metal and coatings, roof geometry, thermal and acoustic behavior, condensation, wind and structural interfaces, rainwater shedding and drainage, fasteners, sealants, flashings, penetrations, corrosion, purchasing, delivery, storage, installation, K3, inspection, repair, replacement, and end of life.

The domain should not be a collection of city-swapped product pages. Geography is excluded from this editorial plan. Existing `/seng/`, `/spandek/`, `/trimdek/`, `/galvalum/`, `/genteng-metal/`, and `/bondek-floor-deck/` routes may retain transactional intent after canonical review. Neutral articles own learning and decision intent. Roof-system guidance does not replace an architect, licensed structural engineer, building-physics specialist, manufacturer, installer, K3 professional, fire consultant, or local authority. No article may infer span, wind capacity, water tightness, coating life, acoustic performance, thermal performance, or safe installation from appearance or a generic product name.

## Evidence and editorial standards

1. Label repository observations, sourced facts, calculations, worked examples, professional judgments, and hypotheses.
2. Cite current primary standards, authority requirements, manufacturer manuals/datasheets, certificates, tested assembly reports, calculations, and original inspections. Record edition, jurisdiction, product/system scope, date, and limitations.
3. Never infer base metal, coating composition/mass, grade, thickness, effective width, span, wind resistance, fire behavior, water tightness, thermal/acoustic performance, or warranty from a generic label.
4. Structural/wind pages require site/building geometry, use, terrain/exposure inputs, governing design basis, pressure zones, product test/design data, supports, load path, fasteners/clips, existing condition, temporary states, and licensed-engineer review.
5. Rain/drainage pages require site rainfall source, roof geometry/slope/length, manufacturer profile/lap details, valleys/gutters/outlets/overflow, discharge destination, blockage/maintenance assumptions, and responsible design.
6. Thermal/acoustic/condensation pages describe the complete tested or modeled assembly and boundary conditions. Isolated product claims cannot be applied to a different roof build-up.
7. Coating/corrosion claims require verified substrate/coating system, environment, cut/formed edges, contact materials, installation damage, underside moisture, maintenance, and warranty conditions.
8. Fastener/sealant/flashing content requires approved compatible components, support substrate, thermal movement, corrosion pairing, manufacturer details, installation controls, and inspection records.
9. Procurement content states dimensions, effective cover, laps, openings, accessories, waste assumptions, product identity, certificates, samples, substitutions, warranty scope, logistics, and acceptance evidence. No undated price or supplier ranking.
10. Installation/K3 content requires a site-specific method, verified bundle/panel information, access/fall/rescue plan, weather criteria, lifting/temporary stability, sharp-edge and hot-work controls, exclusion/public protection, and competent supervision.
11. Inspection distinguishes symptoms from causes. Water near a roof may be rain entry, condensation, plumbing, or another path; structural or severe weather damage triggers competent escalation.
12. Repair/reuse content requires confirmed system/material identity, cause, condition, compatibility, design impact, method, inspection, and warranty implications.
13. Every safety-, structural-, standards-, test-, or price-sensitive page shows author, competent reviewer, sources, review date, and correction route.
14. One page owns one primary same-domain intent. Review title, slug, H1, outline, and Search Console queries before publication.
